## How It Works

### Bidirectional Linking

1. **Craft → Google Drive**: Each section in the Craft document contains a clickable "Open in Google Drive" link
2. **Google Drive → Craft**: You can navigate back from Google Drive to this Craft document via bookmarks
3. **Real-time Awareness**: Changes in Google Drive are immediately visible when you refresh the Craft document

### Usage

1. **Access Folders**: Click any "Open in Google Drive" link to jump directly to the folder
2. **Create Items**: Add files and folders directly in Google Drive
3. **Track Changes**: Refresh the Craft document to see updates
4. **Organize**: Use the Craft document as your primary navigation hub for all five categories
